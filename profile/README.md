## SuperkeyPass

**A family password vault with nothing to remember.**

Open-source and self-hostable. Unlock with your face, fingerprint, or a
security key. Recover with a printed sheet or a trusted family admin. Get
alerted if someone grabs your unlocked device, and get told exactly which
passwords to change.
